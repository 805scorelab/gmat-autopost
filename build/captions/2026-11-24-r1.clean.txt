28% of 75 without a calculator? Swap the numbers and it takes 5 seconds.

28% of 75 is the same as 75% of 28, because both are 28 times 75 divided by 100. And 75% is three quarters. Split 28 into 4 parts of 7, keep 3 of them, and you get 21. The answer is B.

It works on any pair: 8% of 50 is 50% of 8, which is 4. Use it whenever one of the two numbers is a friendly percent like 25, 50 or 75.

Percent questions show up all over GMAT quant, and there is no calculator in that section. Save this for your next percent question.
