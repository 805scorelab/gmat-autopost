What is the last digit of 7 to the power 23? No calculator needed.

Powers of 7 end in 7, 9, 3, 1 and then repeat. The cycle is 4 long, and 23 divided by 4 leaves 3, so the answer is the 3rd digit: 3. The answer is B.

The trap answer is C. A power of 7 ends in 7 only once every 4 powers.
