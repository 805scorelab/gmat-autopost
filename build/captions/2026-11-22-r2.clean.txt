4 numbers average 10. Add 1 more and the average jumps to 12. What did you add?

The trap answer is 12. The fix is to stop working with averages and switch to totals. 4 numbers averaging 10 sum to 40. 5 numbers averaging 12 sum to 60. The new number is 60 minus 40, which is 20. The answer is C.

Faster still: the new number pays for its own 12, plus 2 extra for each of the other 4. That's 12 + 8 = 20.

Averages are core GMAT quant, and totals make them easy. Save this one.
