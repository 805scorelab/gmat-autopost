You get 2 equations and want x + y. Do you need both? The trap answer is C.

Statement 1 is 2 times x plus 2 times y = 14, which is 2 times (x + y) = 14. Divide by 2 and x + y = 7, whatever x and y are. Sufficient. Statement 2 says x - y = 1, and pairs like 5 and 4 or 1 and 0 give different sums. Not sufficient. The answer is A.

Data sufficiency sits in the Data Insights section of the GMAT. When the question asks for a combination like x + y, solve for that combination, not for every letter.
