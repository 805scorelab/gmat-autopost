What is the remainder when 5,738 is divided by 9? Skip the long division.

Add the digits: 5 + 7 + 3 + 8 = 23, then 2 + 3 = 5. A number leaves the same remainder by 9 as its digit sum. The answer is C.

The trap answer is E. The last digit, 8, is the remainder for 10, not for 9.
