5 consecutive integers add up to 85. What is the largest one?

This is an original GMAT quant problem from 805 Score Lab. Pick A to E on slide 3 before you swipe.

The move: with an odd count, the middle number is the average. Slide 4 finds it in 1 step, 85 ÷ 5 = 17.

Slide 8 shows the trap answer, 17. It is the middle of the list, and the question asks for the top.
