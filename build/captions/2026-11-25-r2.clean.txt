A gym asks its 6 a.m. classes when members like to train. Almost all say mornings. What is wrong with the conclusion?

All 40 dots in the Reel stand for members; only the 6 a.m. group was asked. Members outside the 6 a.m. classes never got the question, yet the conclusion covers all members. The answer is A.

The trap answer is C. Almost all of a group is always most of it, so that step is fine. The leap is from 1 class to the whole gym.
