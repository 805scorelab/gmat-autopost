Pens cost $2, books cost $5, and you buy at least 1 of each. How many pens?

Statement 1 alone ($19 spent) allows 7 pens with 1 book or 2 pens with 3 books. Statement 2 alone (5 items) allows any split. Together only 2 pens fit, so the answer is C.

The trap answer is A. Whole numbers can still leave 2 answers, so always test a second case.
