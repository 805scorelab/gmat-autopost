Data Sufficiency, expectation vs reality.

In DS you never need the value itself. You only need to know whether it can be found.

Example: statement (1) says x + y = 10 and statement (2) says 2 times (x + y) = 20. Divide (2) by 2 and you get (1) again, so (2) adds nothing.

Once you know a single value exists, stop solving and pick your letter. Those saved minutes go to the harder questions in Data Insights.
