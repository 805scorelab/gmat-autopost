A price went up. By what percent? (1) It rose by $12. (2) The new price is $72.

Statement 1 alone fails: $12 on $60 is 20%, but $12 on $40 is 30%. Statement 2 alone gives no old price either.

Together, the old price is 72 - 12 = 60, and 12 out of 60 is 20%. The answer is C.

The trap answer is E. You never see the old price, but new minus increase gives it.
