In 5 years, Ana will be twice as old as she was 7 years ago. How old is she now?

This is an original GMAT quant age word problem from 805 Score Lab. Pick A to E on slide 3 before you swipe.

The move on slide 5: "twice what she was" doubles her whole past age, so the 2 multiplies the 7 too.

Slide 9 shows the trap answer, 12. It doubles only a and lands on her age 7 years ago.
