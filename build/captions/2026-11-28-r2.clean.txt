Is the integer n odd? (1) 3n is odd. (2) n + n is even.

Statement 1 is sufficient: 3 x 4 = 12 is even and 3 x 5 = 15 is odd, so an odd 3n means n is odd.

Statement 2 rules nothing out, since 4 + 4 = 8 and 5 + 5 = 10 are both even. The answer is A.

The trap answer is D. Statement 2 is true for every integer, so it gives you no information.
