A pool cut its fee in half and got 40% more visits. So why did it earn less?

This is an original GMAT Critical Reasoning inference question from 805 Score Lab. Pick A to E on slide 3 first.

The test on slide 5: half the fee times 1.4 times the visits is 0.7 of last summer's income. Only 1 choice stays inside those facts.

Slide 8 shows the trap answer, E. A "should" brings in a plan the statements never give.
