Darnell doubled its cyclists on Main Street with bike lanes. Will lanes double cycling on every street?

This is a GMAT critical reasoning weaken question, and it's original to 805 Score Lab. Read the argument on slide 3 and pick A to E before you swipe.

The move for weaken questions: find the gap between the evidence and the conclusion. Here the evidence is 1 street. The conclusion is about every street. Slides 4 and 5 map that gap in 2 steps.
