Fernhill Café launched a loyalty app and spending per visit rose 15%. Did the app cause it?

This is an original GMAT Critical Reasoning weaken question from 805 Score Lab. Pick A to E on slide 3 first.

The test on slide 5: look for something else that changed in March. A 15% price rise explains the same numbers with no help from the app.

Slide 8 shows the trap answer, E. Lots of downloads makes the app story more likely, not less.
