Boys to girls is 3 to 5. Then 6 boys join and it's 3 to 4. How many girls are in the class?

This is an original GMAT quant ratio problem from 805 Score Lab. Pick A to E on slide 3 before you swipe.

The move: call one part k. Boys are 3k, girls are 5k, and the girls never change. Slide 5 turns the new ratio into 1 equation and finds k = 8.

Slide 8 shows the trap answer, and it catches people who solve everything right and then answer the wrong question.
