28 take French, 20 take Spanish, 9 take both. How many of the 50 take neither?

This is an original GMAT quant overlapping sets problem from 805 Score Lab. Pick A to E on slide 3 before you swipe.

The move: adding 28 and 20 counts the 9 students in both groups twice. Slide 5 removes them once to get 39.

Slide 8 shows the trap answer, 2. It forgets the double count.
