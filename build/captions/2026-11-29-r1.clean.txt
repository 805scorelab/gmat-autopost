A jacket costs $80 after 20% off. What was the price before the discount?

Cut the old price into 5 blocks of 20%. You pay for 4 of them, so 4 blocks are $80, 1 block is $20 and the old price is $100. The answer is D.

The trap answer is C. $96 adds 20% of $80, but the discount was taken from the old price.
