Every intern who finishes the training gets an offer. Sam got none. What must be true?

Write the rule as 1 arrow: finished training leads to an offer. If Sam had finished, Sam would have an offer. Sam has none, so Sam did not finish. The answer is A.

The trap answer is C. It flips the arrow and claims every intern with an offer finished the training. The rule never says that.

Must be true questions are part of GMAT Critical Reasoning. The Verbal section gives you 45 minutes for 23 questions, so spotting a flipped arrow in 10 seconds pays off. Save this for your next Critical Reasoning set.
