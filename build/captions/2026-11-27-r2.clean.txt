A school banned phones in class in September. By December, scores rose. Did the ban do it?

The argument names 1 cause for the rise. Choice B adds a 2nd: daily tutoring began in every class that same fall. The answer is B.

The trap answer is E. The ban covers class time, so phones at lunch give no other cause.

Weaken questions are part of GMAT Critical Reasoning in the Verbal section. Before you accept a cause, ask what else changed at the same time. Save this for your next CR set.
