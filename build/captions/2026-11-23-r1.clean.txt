60 mph out, 40 mph back. Your average speed isn't 50.

Drive 120 miles at 60 and you're on the road for 2 hours. Drive back at 40 and it takes 3 hours. That's 240 miles in 5 hours: 48 mph. The answer is B.

You spend more time at the slower speed, so the average leans toward 40. For equal distances, the shortcut is 2 times 60 times 40, divided by 100: 48.
