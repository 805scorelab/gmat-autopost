Machine A takes 6 hours. A and B together take 4. How long does B take alone?

This is an original GMAT quant work rate problem from 805 Score Lab. Pick A to E on slide 3 before you swipe.

The move: hours can't be subtracted, but rates can. Slide 5 turns 1/4 minus 1/6 into 1/12 of a batch per hour.

Slide 8 shows the trap answer, 6 minus 4. It looks fast and makes machine B 3 times quicker than A.
