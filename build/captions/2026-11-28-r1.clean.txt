5 numbers average 12. Remove 1 and the other 4 average 10. Which number did you remove?

Turn each average into a total: 5 x 12 = 60 and 4 x 10 = 40. The gap, 60 - 40 = 20, is the number removed. The answer is D.

The trap answer is A. 2 is how far the average dropped, not the number you took out.
