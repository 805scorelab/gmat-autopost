# The current GMAT exam (checked 21 Nov 2026; re-check on the first Monday of every month)

Only what is written here may be taught as an exam rule. If GMAC changes anything, this file changes first and no
post may contradict it. Primary sources first; secondary sources are marked and used only where GMAC's own page is silent.

## Structure
| Section | Questions | Time | Question types | Calculator |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Quantitative Reasoning | 21 | 45 min | Problem Solving only (5 choices); arithmetic and elementary algebra | No |
| Verbal Reasoning | 23 | 45 min | Reading Comprehension, Critical Reasoning | No |
| Data Insights | 20 | 45 min | Data Sufficiency, Multi-Source Reasoning, Table Analysis, Graphics Interpretation, Two-Part Analysis | Yes, on-screen |

- 64 questions, 2 hours 15 minutes of testing, one optional 10-minute break. [1][2][3]
- Sections may be taken in any order (6 possible orders). [2][3]
- Review and edit: you may review any question in a section and change up to 3 answers per section. [3]
- Computer adaptive. [secondary: Kaplan]

## Scores
- Total score 205 to 805, in 10-point steps. All three sections weigh equally. [1][2]
- Section scores 60 to 90, in 1-point steps. [2][3]
- GMAT Superscore: announced by GMAC on 16 Jun 2026, live from Aug 2026; computes a candidate's best performance across
  attempts of the current edition. [4] Describe it only in those words; do not explain mechanics beyond GMAC's page.

## What is NOT on the exam (never teach as current)
- Geometry (Euclidean) is gone from Quant. Coordinate-plane algebra (slopes, lines) can still appear as algebra.
  [GMAC: Quant covers "arithmetic and elementary algebra" [1]; secondary: Manhattan Review, e-GMAT]
- Sentence Correction is gone; Verbal is Reading Comprehension and Critical Reasoning only. [1]
- Data Sufficiency is no longer in Quant; it lives in Data Insights. [1]
- No Analytical Writing Assessment, no Integrated Reasoning section (replaced by Data Insights). [2]

## Naming
- The exam was launched as the "GMAT Focus Edition" (Nov 2023); the classic GMAT retired 31 Jan 2024; GMAC later dropped
  "Focus Edition" and calls it simply the GMAT exam. [secondary: Magoosh, updated 2026]
- On the page: say "GMAT". "GMAT Focus" may appear as a search phrase in captions, because people still search it.

## Our rules that follow from this
- Quant items: Problem Solving, 5 choices, no calculator needed, no geometry.
- Data Sufficiency items carry the standard 5 fixed answer statements and sit under Data Insights.
- DI items may assume an on-screen calculator; still keep numbers clean enough for a phone screen.
- Timing advice: 45 minutes for 21 Quant items is about 2 minutes 8 seconds each; for 23 Verbal, about 1 minute 57 seconds;
  for 20 DI, 2 minutes 15 seconds. (Arithmetic from the official counts and times, not a GMAC recommendation.)
- Anything else dated (fees, test-centre rules, percentiles, school averages) must be looked up the same day and cited,
  or left as {{placeholder}}.
